# Personal Life Compass – Product Prompt

I want you to act as a Senior Product Designer, UX Designer, and Full Stack Engineer.

Build a minimal, elegant web application called **Life Compass**.

This is NOT a productivity app.
This is NOT a habit tracker.
This is NOT a note-taking application.

Its purpose is to remind me every day of the person I am becoming and help me stay aligned with that vision whenever I feel unmotivated or lost.

The application should feel calm, premium, minimal, and distraction-free.

Think Apple-level simplicity.

---

## Design Philosophy

Every screen should answer one question:

**"Am I living like the person I want to become?"**

The application should never overwhelm me with too much information.

Show only what is necessary.

Everything else should stay hidden until I choose to view it.

# DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

Use lots of whitespace.

Dark mode by default.

Beautiful typography.

Rounded cards.

Smooth animations.

Minimal icons.

No clutter.

No dashboards full of charts.

No unnecessary statistics.

No productivity pressure.

The application should feel peaceful.

Opening it should feel like talking to my wiser future self.

Every design decision should reinforce one principle:

"This application exists to help me become the best version of myself, not the busiest version of myself."
